01 / THE IDEA

It's okay
not to get it
yet.

The right explanation can change everything.

Math Unlocked is built around a simple idea: students deserve access to supportive tutoring, and tutors deserve a place to share what they know. We're creating a bridge between the two — so asking for help becomes a first step, not a big deal.

06 / QUICK QUESTIONS

Before
you jump in.

I'm a student. Which form do I use?

Use the student waitlist form. It helps the team understand what kind of maths support you're looking for.

I'm interested in tutoring. Where do I apply?

Use the tutor application form. That's the separate route for people who want to become Math Unlocked tutors.

Do I need to know exactly what I need help with?

No. Share what you can in the student form. You can describe the topics or areas where you'd like support.

Where can I connect with the community?

Join the Math Unlocked Discord community to connect and follow updates.
